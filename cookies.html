Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your computer, phone or other device when you visit a website. Cookies and similar technologies can be used for purposes such as remembering preferences, maintaining website functionality, measuring visitor activity or delivering advertising. Some cookies are essential to the operation of a service, while others require your consent under applicable law. ## 3.
